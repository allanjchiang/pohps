Support

POHPS is a free, open-source app designed to help lacto-ovo vegetarians track daily protein and optional water intake, with no ads. Core tracking is fully free. An optional subscription, POHPS Pro, unlocks a Statistics area (trend graphs, a goal calendar, and Excel export) — see the Subscriptions section below.

How to get help

  • Email allan@logicphile.com
  • Include your device model, Android version, and app version (Settings → About POHPS)
  • Describe what you were trying to do and what happened

We aim to respond within a few business days. We cannot provide medical or dietary advice by email—please speak with your doctor or registered dietitian for personal health guidance.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Logicphile Limited (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how the POHPS mobile application (com.logicphile.pohps) handles information.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data on our servers.
  • All app data is stored locally on your device only using Android’s app storage.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • There are no accounts, logins, or cloud sync.
  • The release app does not require network access for its features.

Data stored on your device

May include:

  • Daily protein and water intake logs
  • Your daily protein and water goals and app preferences (language, units, diet type, theme)
  • Custom foods you create and optional protein overrides for certain items
  • Achievement progress stored locally
  • Whether you accepted the in-app disclaimer
  • For POHPS Pro: your free-trial start date and a locally cached record of which subscription plan is active, so the app can show Statistics without contacting a server

This data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data in your device settings. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

Data we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or address (unless you voluntarily email support)
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Health data transmitted to our servers (we have no servers for app data)
  • Payment card, billing address, or other payment details — POHPS Pro purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never see or store your payment information

Support emails

If you contact allan@logicphile.com, we receive only what you choose to send (e.g. your email address and message content). We use that information only to respond to your request and do not sell it to third parties.

Children

POHPS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

Subscriptions (POHPS Pro)

All core protein and water tracking in POHPS is free, with no ads. An optional subscription, POHPS Pro, unlocks a Statistics area: protein trend graphs (Week/Month/Year/Year to Date), a calendar that highlights days you met your goal, and Excel export of your log.

Free trial

New users get a 30-day free trial of POHPS Pro, handled entirely on your device — no payment method is required to start the trial.

Billing

After the trial, POHPS Pro is billed monthly or annually, at the price shown in the app before you buy, through the Apple App Store or Google Play (whichever store you installed POHPS from). Your subscription automatically renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You will be charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends.

Managing or canceling

Manage or cancel your subscription anytime in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions. Canceling stops future renewals; it does not refund the current period.

Refunds

All charges are final and non-refundable, except where required by law in your country (for example, statutory consumer withdrawal rights in the EU, UK, and elsewhere). Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google, not by Logicphile Limited directly, since we never receive your payment.

Questions about your subscription? Contact allan@logicphile.com.
